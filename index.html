Legal services

Data Protection

Lawfulness · Fairness · Transparency

Data protection law governs how organisations collect, use, store and share personal information. Most jurisdictions now require organisations to process personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently, to keep it secure, and to respect the rights of the people it concerns.

Legal Lounge assists organisations to understand and meet these obligations. The work includes assessing existing practices against the applicable law, preparing privacy policies and notices, drafting data processing and data sharing agreements, advising on cross-border transfers of personal data, and training staff on their responsibilities.

  • Compliance assessments
  • Privacy policies and notices
  • Data protection training

Company Registrations

Separate legal personality

Incorporation gives a business its own legal personality, separate from the people who own and run it. The process, the documents required and the continuing obligations differ from one jurisdiction to another.

Legal Lounge handles the registration of companies and other business entities in Zimbabwe and in foreign jurisdictions. This includes name searches and reservations, preparation of constitutional documents, registration of foreign companies and branches, and the statutory filings and annual returns that keep an entity in good standing.

  • Companies and PBCs
  • Foreign entities and branches
  • Statutory filings and annual returns

Law and Information Technology

Law at the intersection of technology and regulation

Technology raises legal questions that traditional areas of law do not always answer directly: how contracts are formed online, how electronic evidence is treated, what amounts to a cyber offence, and how digital services are regulated.

Legal Lounge advises on the legal aspects of information technology, including software, licensing and service agreements, cyber law, electronic transactions and regulatory compliance for digital businesses.

  • Technology agreements
  • Cyber law
  • Digital regulatory compliance

Intellectual Property

Protection of creative and commercial works

Intellectual property law protects the products of the mind: brands, creative works, inventions and designs. Rights such as trade marks and copyright allow their owners to control how those works are used and to act against infringement.

Legal Lounge advises on the protection and management of intellectual property rights, including trade mark registration, copyright and neighbouring rights, licensing arrangements and the enforcement of rights against infringement.

  • Trade mark registration
  • Copyright and neighbouring rights
  • Enforcement of rights

Case Law Review

Stare decisis

The doctrine of precedent requires courts to follow the reasoning of earlier decisions on the same point of law. Understanding a judgment means identifying its ratio decidendi and tracing how later courts have applied, distinguished or departed from it.

Legal Lounge reviews and analyses judgments for practitioners, organisations and researchers. Each review is prepared as a written memorandum setting out the facts, the issues, the reasoning of the court and the significance of the decision, with comparative review across jurisdictions where required.

  • Case digests
  • Precedent analysis
  • Comparative review across jurisdictions

Legal Research

Statute · Precedent · Scholarship

Sound legal analysis rests on thorough research into the relevant statutes, case law and scholarly writing.

Legal Lounge undertakes research on questions of law for practitioners, organisations and institutions. The work is presented as research memoranda and legislative analyses, and can support the preparation of legal opinions, submissions and policy work.

  • Research memoranda
  • Legislative analysis
  • Research in support of legal opinions

Community

Alongside its legal practice, Legal Lounge convenes the legal profession through the Forum, its mooting programme and its publishing house.

The Forum

Audi alteram partem

The Legal Lounge Forum is a network for legal professionals across jurisdictions. It was created to give lawyers a place to connect, exchange knowledge and grow professionally alongside one another.

The Forum hosts networking events, seminars and webinars on developments in the law, and encourages collaboration between practitioners in different countries. It is open to practising lawyers, academics and legal professionals at every stage of their careers.

  • Networking
  • Seminars and webinars
  • Cross-border exchange

Mooting

Docendo discimus · By teaching, we learn

A moot is a simulated court hearing in which participants argue a point of law before a bench, as they would in an appeal. Mooting develops the skills of legal research, written submissions and oral advocacy.

Legal Lounge runs moot court competitions for law students and young practitioners. Participants prepare and argue cases before benches of practising lawyers, and the programme includes training in research and advocacy.

  • Moot court competitions
  • Advocacy training
  • Benches of practitioners

Publishing House

Scholarship in the service of the law

Legal Lounge maintains a publishing house for legal scholarship. It publishes articles, case notes and commentary on developments in the law.

The publishing house issues periodic calls for papers, open to practitioners, academics and students. Submissions are reviewed before publication, and the publishing house also prepares practice guides on selected areas of law.

  • Calls for papers
  • Articles and case notes
  • Practice guides
